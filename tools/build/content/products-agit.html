The writing desk that remembers your story

By chapter twelve, every writer learns the same lesson: the story outgrows your head. The protagonist's eye colour quietly changes, the subplot you swore you closed is still open, and the research tab you need died in a browser crash an hour ago.

AGIT is a free desktop writing studio that takes over the remembering. The manuscript, the outline, the notes, the story bible and the AI helper live in one workspace on your own computer — and the computer does the recall. Ask it who owns the tavern in chapter three. It knows. It read the chapter.

There is no subscription, there is no account, and your book never has to leave your machine. You can write a whole novel on a plane.

One desk, the whole project

Chat on one side, the manuscript in the middle, the outline and boards a click away. The AI chat is not a stranger: it reads your project, so "does this contradict chapter four?" is a real question with a real answer.

Everything autosaves, snapshots keep yesterday's version of every scene, and the draft exports to DOCX, EPUB, PDF, Markdown or plain text whenever you feel like holding it.

Your files, your machine

The web stays in your house

Projects, notes and research sit in a folder on your own disk — Documents\AGIT by default, movable whenever you like. Autosave and snapshots stand guard. Uninstalling the app never deletes your writing; the folder is yours.

Want a backup in the cloud? Sync runs through your own Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive account, encrypted before it leaves the machine. We never hold your files hostage, because we never hold your files.

Telemetry is opt-in, asked once, quiet afterwards. No advertising trackers, no social pixels. The privacy policy is short on purpose.

AI on your terms

Bring your own API key for the big cloud models, or run a local model through Ollama or an imported GGUF file and keep every token on your machine. Different jobs can use different models: the clever one for drafting, a cheap one for the chores. Usage tracking keeps the bill visible before it becomes a surprise.

Cloud models stay switched off until you configure them. The writing itself works fully offline.

Questions people actually ask

Is my novel sent anywhere?

No. Writing, planning and export are fully offline. The only things that ever touch the network are the AI providers you configure yourself, the optional sync to your own cloud storage, and opt-in error reporting that starts switched off.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

It is free to use and free to share under the AG Free Use Licence — free for any purpose, and the source stays with AG. There is no trial timer, no feature paywall, no credit meter inside the app. The studio sells nothing here — this page is the storefront.

Will it run on my computer?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, about 2 GB of disk space. That is the whole list. macOS builds are planned; today AGIT is honest about being a Windows citizen.

What if I want to leave?

Export everything to DOCX, EPUB, PDF, HTML, Markdown or plain text and walk away with your words. An app should earn its keep every day you open it — not keep your book as collateral.
