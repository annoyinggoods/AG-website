Small games, tiny WASM

The playground is where the studio's small things live: little games and tiny WebAssembly projects that are too small for a product page of their own and too playful for the labs. Every game in here is real and playable on this site, and each one has a product page of its own with the download.

The games

  • drAGhts — chaotic checkers with eight toggleable house rules. Play a friend hot-seat or face the computer.
  • nAG — a reverse-order memory game. Scout the board, then replay the whole order in one perfect run.
  • AGnosis — the ASCII duck parade, live in your browser as WebAssembly. Poke the ducks, bring your own art, export the parade.

A layout of their own

Every game page drops the usual site dressing. The spider ball rests in the top-left corner and never animates; the menu sits on the left; the title box moves to the right, just above the main box. The main box itself is a solid black stage — fully opaque in both themes, at least 720 pixels wide and tall, with no glass panel behind it — so a game or a WASM canvas has a clean surface to sit on.
