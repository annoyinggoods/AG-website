Small games, tiny WASM

The playground is where the studio's small things live: little games and tiny WebAssembly projects that are too small for a product page of their own and too playful for the labs. It has four slots, one subpage each, and every slot is a real page already — the games will move in as they are built.

The four slots

  • Slot One — reserved for the first game or experiment.
  • Slot Two — reserved for the second game or experiment.
  • Slot Three — reserved for the third game or experiment.
  • Slot Four — reserved for the fourth game or experiment.

A layout of their own

Every slot page drops the usual site dressing. The spider ball rests in the top-left corner and never animates; the menu sits on the left; the title box moves to the right, just above the main box. The main box itself is a solid black stage — fully opaque in both themes, at least 720 pixels wide and tall, with no glass panel behind it — so a game or a WASM canvas has a clean surface to sit on.
