FAQ

The short answers

Plain questions with plain answers. If yours is not here, ask through the contact page.

What is AGIT

AGIT is the studio's writing and research companion. It keeps drafts, outlines, boards, and source notes in one local-first workspace.

Your words stay in plain files on your own machine, where you can copy them, back them up, and keep them.

What products do you make

Four. AGIT is the flagship writing tool. AGrrr! is the page-builder for the raw first pass of a website. AGnosis is the hotkey daemon that hides your browser behind a duck parade. AGDevelop is the studio workbench behind the experiments.

Only AGDevelop is still a content shell. The products page lists all four and says which is which.

What does the cookie banner do

It appears on your first visit so you can see, in plain words, what the site stores and why.

Press OK to accept, or EXIT to decline. Either answer closes the banner and stops it coming back. The footer carries a Cookie settings link that re-opens it whenever you want to change your mind.

Where are my cookie preferences stored

In your own browser, under the key ag-cookie-consent. The record never leaves your device, and the studio cannot read it from here.

Clear your browser storage and the banner will ask you again the next time you arrive.

How do I report a bug

Use the report bug form. Give the product, a one line summary, and the steps that reproduce the problem.

What you expected and what actually happened help. A screen recording helps more than a description, but plain steps are fine. The studio reads every report.

How do I request a feature

Use the request a feature form. Name the product, give the idea a title, and describe the use case.

One clear idea per message. Long essays arrive as three separate ideas, and only the first one gets read carefully.

What does the Compute and Chart demo do

You paste text into a box. The demo counts characters, words, sentences, and unique words, then lists the terms that appear most and draws them as a small chart.

The Compute and Chart page shows the same thing with a full explanation.

Does the demo send my text anywhere

No. The counting happens in your browser, on your machine. The text is not uploaded, not logged, and not sent to the studio or to anyone else.

Close the tab and it is gone. You can check with the network panel open, if you enjoy that sort of thing.

How do I reach the studio

Through the two forms. One is for bugs, one is for features, and both land in the studio mailbox on this domain.

The contact page routes you to the closer match. There is no phone number and no chat window.

What does the site store about me

Only what you type into a form, and only if you send it yourself. On top of that, the site remembers a few settings in your own browser: your theme, your cookie answer, where you parked the menu bubble, which menu branches you left open, and whether you have already seen the intro animation.

The privacy policy lists every stored key in full. The terms of use cover the rules for everything else.
