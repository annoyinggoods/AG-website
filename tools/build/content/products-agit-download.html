AGIT is free to use and free to pass to your writing group, under the AG Free Use Licence — free for any purpose, and the source stays with AG. No trial timer, no feature paywall, no credit meter — the whole studio ships in one installer.

Download the AGIT 1.0.1 installer (534 MB) for Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Run it, follow the wizard, and start writing; your projects live in Documents\AGIT and survive both updates and uninstalls.

Before you double-click

  • SmartScreen may introduce itself. Direct downloads sometimes trigger a Windows SmartScreen notice for unsigned installers — choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
  • What it needs: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and about 2 GB of disk. No account, no online activation.
  • Where your writing goes: Documents\AGIT — a normal folder you can back up, move or sync yourself. Uninstalling the app never deletes it.
  • What phones home: nothing by default. Telemetry is opt-in and starts switched off; AI providers connect only after you add keys; sync runs through your own cloud accounts.

First steps after installing are covered in the FAQ; the honest state of the current build lives in release notes. When something misbehaves, the bug form lands it on the workbench.
