AGnosis help

AGnosis runs two ways, and each has its own short guide. Pick yours: the browser version runs right here on the site with nothing to install, and the desktop app puts a hotkey on your machine that hides the browsers behind a fullscreen duck parade. The full read-me — every command, every config key, byte for byte what the app itself shows when you press F1 — is the third door.

In the browser

Start the parade on the playground page, poke the ducks, draw your own duck in the built-in editor and take the result with you as a download. No install, no account, nothing leaves the page. The short guide →

On the desktop

Install the app on Windows, macOS or Linux, press the hotkey and every open browser minimizes behind the parade. This guide covers the install, every key, the editor, the config file and the exports — with the honest answers to the questions about privacy and platforms at the bottom. The desktop guide →
