Prototypes

Rough, disposable, one question

A prototype exists to answer a single question. When the question is answered, the prototype is usually thrown away, even when it worked.

Nothing here is finished. Parts appear, move, and vanish between one visit and the next. Nothing on this page is a promise about a product.
