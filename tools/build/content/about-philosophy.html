Philosophy

Horror-comedy with a work ethic

We believe writing tools should feel alive, a little creepy, and completely yours.

No accounts forced on you.

No data harvesting dressed up as convenience.

Local first, plain formats, and files you can keep forever.

Our stance on AI:

AI is a tool and it is as good as the user's skill in wielding it. We don't vibe code our products, not because we're against it but because most of what you see has been made to learn, for fun and because AI subscriptions that would allow amount of tokens needed for building this whole thing would be very expensive. We stick to $15 a month total AI expenditure- that is enough to troubleshoot, run automated audits and request edge-case testing for features.

Our stance on Open Source:

If you read our licenses you will notice we do not adhere to OSI rules. Whenever possible we use modified Hippocratic license- for all intended purposes source-available Hippocratic license is highly permissive unless you are associated with military, genocide, environmental crimes or Human Rights abuse in which case you are not allowed to touch the software or it's code and please leave this website now.

It's not that we think our code can actually be useful to hurt people, we just don't believe people who do objectively shit things and discriminate against others should be welcomed with open arms.

Our stance on Code:

We like when code is humane. It's not always possible to make everything easy to read but we try to avoid nesting loops, nesting ifs, deep nesting in general. Comments about functionality of code are praised and their lack is treated as a bug, and yes, our software has a few bugs- everything you see here, as of November 2026, still needs more testing by actual users- please download stuff and help us get to the finish line sooner! Every bug reported is a step forward to great software

Our stance on Aesthetics:

Our team doesn't have a designer (or anyone with an impeccable taste for that matter) so WYSIWYG unless you suggest improvements.

We don't think our visuals are bad, but there is an inkling in our hearts we could do better if we knew what we are doing and where to aim. We tried the viral Qt visual improvement skill for AI on AGIT and honestly, apart from a few suggestions on colour contrast for themes it did not offer any useful insights so either we already did well or AI has even worse sense of taste than us. We hope it's the latter one.
