AGnosis

One hotkey on your desktop and every open browser minimizes behind a fullscreen ASCII duck parade. This is that same parade, right here on the page: the simulation core runs in your browser as WebAssembly. Click a duck and it jumps; shift-click holds it. Bring your own art (a .agnosis package or a manifest the native app exported), draw your own ducks in the built-in editor, record the parade as a self-contained page or an animated GIF, and take the art back out as a package. Nothing leaves your browser — the recordings are made on the page, not on a server.

draw with the mouse — a drag is one undo step · type the name in the top bar · SAVE downloads the document · ESC returns to the parade · the short guide
