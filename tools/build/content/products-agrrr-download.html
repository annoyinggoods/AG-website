Before you double-click

  • SmartScreen may introduce itself. Direct downloads sometimes trigger a Windows SmartScreen notice for unsigned installers — choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
  • What it needs: a 64-bit Windows machine. No account, no online activation, nothing to subscribe to.
  • Where your projects go: in folders you choose on your own disk — page source as plain files, metadata in a local database beside them. Uninstalling the app never deletes them.
  • What it phones home: nothing. The core never makes a network call; the network is used only when you plug in an AI key of your own choosing, or when you deploy to the host you own.
