AGrrr! next to the field

Comparisons are usually written by the winner, so here is our attempt at the honest version. The field this time is four other ways to get a website: Lovable, the IONOS Website Builder, WIX, and writing the code yourself. They are all good roads. They are also different bargains — different ideas about where your site lives, who pays every month, and what happens when the meter runs.

AGrrr!'s difference starts before AI enters the room. It ships a large and growing library of ready-made scripts and components — more than 1,200 records of pre-validated HTML, CSS and JavaScript — that need no AI at all: you place them, you fill in your own words, and they work offline. When you do want a model, AI is optional and bring-your-own-key — any provider, your account, your pricing — with no tie to one company's model, bill or terms of service.

One honest note before the table: competitor tools change monthly, so their cells are phrased from your standpoint as the user and kept general, and where a cell depends on what a vendor offers right now it says as of writing. Check their pages for the current state, and check ours too.

The four, in their own terms

Lovable — the AI app factory

You describe the site or app in a chat, the service builds it, and you keep iterating by conversation until it looks right. For getting from a sentence to a working, hosted product fast, it is genuinely impressive. The road itself is the AI: the platform, the generation and the hosting are one service with one account, your project lives in their cloud, and the code can be exported if you want it out — as of writing. lovable.dev.

The IONOS Website Builder — the bundle from your host

A guided website editor that comes with IONOS hosting contracts, so the domain, the mail, the hosting and the builder sit on one bill. For a small site that must simply exist, it is a fair bargain, and support is a phone call. The road into the builder has narrowed, though: manual building is very restricted today, and the compositor leans heavily on its AI to produce the site. The free tier meters that AI by the day and the month — the allowance is solid enough that you will get a working website out of it, but iterating and improving beyond it is where the free road gets thin, as of writing. The builder is tied to the contract: the machinery stays theirs, and moving the site elsewhere means rebuilding it in another tool. ionos.com.

WIX — the big template marketplace

An enormous ecosystem: hundreds of templates, an app market for every add-on, a drag-and-drop editor, and a free tier that costs nothing but carries their branding. You rent the platform — the site is served from WIX's infrastructure, the account and the plan are theirs, and taking their branding off means paying monthly, as of writing. For template breadth alone, nothing on this page matches it. wix.com.

Writing the code yourself — the honest bench

Total control, zero lock-in, no bill but the hosting, and the site is yours the moment the first file is saved. The price is real study and real time: HTML, CSS and JavaScript are trades, not features, and every builder on this page is selling you a shortcut past this bench. AGrrr!'s pitch is a shorter bench — the code is written for you, but it stays real, plain and yours to edit when curiosity strikes.

Where the others win

No honest comparison skips this section.

Lovable wins the race from sentence to product

There is no AGrrr! mode where you describe the site, walk away, and come back to a finished thing — by design, since a composition you did not make is a site you cannot account for. If the fastest possible first draft matters more than knowing every placement, Lovable's road is shorter, and its iteration loop is genuinely quick.

IONOS wins the one-bill simplicity

Domain, mail, hosting and builder on a single contract with a support hotline is a real convenience, and AGrrr! assumes you already have hosting rather than selling it to you. If you want one invoice and one phone number, the bundle is the better bargain.

WIX wins breadth, and it is not close

Hundreds of templates and an app market for every niche dwarf any single library, however loud AGrrr!'s is. If the design you want already exists as a WIX template and the app market covers the rest, rebuilding it from blocks is work you do not need to do.

Writing it yourself wins the ceiling

No tool's limits are yours when the tool is you. AGrrr!'s records, regions and rules are broad but bounded — a composition the compiler refuses will stay refused until the app grows the rule — and hand-written code simply has no such wall. What AGrrr! offers is a shorter road to the same plain files, not a wider one.

And the honest list about AGrrr!

  • It is Windows-only today, and the early installer is exactly that — early, with sharp edges a release build will have sanded off.
  • It is a single-user app. There is no team editing, no co-authoring, no client login to a workspace.
  • The library is large but bounded. If the block you need is not there and you cannot write it, the wall in the previous section applies to you too.
  • You still answer the small-screen proposals yourself. The scaling engine proposes; it does not ship until you have decided every question.
  • It is closed source and free, not open source — the licence carries ethical-use rules, which is a different bargain from the MIT rows elsewhere on this page.

The short version

Who each road is for

  • Lovable if you want to describe a product and walk away, and a subscription with credits on their cloud sounds fair for it.
  • The IONOS Website Builder if you already have, or want, IONOS hosting and one bill, and you are happy to build the way its AI steers — solid first results, with further iteration metered on the free tier (as of writing).
  • WIX if the template you want already exists, the app market covers the rest, and renting the platform is a price you accept for the breadth.
  • Writing it yourself if the craft is the point, the ceiling must be yours, and the evenings are free.
  • AGrrr! if you want to place ready-made blocks that work with no AI, keep the whole site as plain files on your own machine, put it on any host you like — and, when you do want a model, bring your own key and answer to nobody's subscription.
