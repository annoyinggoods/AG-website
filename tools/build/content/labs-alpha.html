Alpha

Usable, unstable, sharp edges

An alpha build works well enough to be worth using. It is not finished, and it is not guarded.

Features may change, move, or disappear without warning. Work you do in an alpha is not a promise of anything. Keep a copy of anything you care about.

Rewrite panel

An in-editor panel that offers several rewrites of a paragraph and lets you keep the one you want.

Status: alpha

Source view

A side panel that keeps every source note visible while the draft is open.

Status: alpha

Scene board

A board that arranges scenes as cards you can drag into a new order.

Status: alpha

Tell the spider what broke

Alpha builds are the most useful thing you can report. Send a bug report when something snaps, or a feature request when something is missing.
